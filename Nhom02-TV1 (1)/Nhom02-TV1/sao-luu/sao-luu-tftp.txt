! =====================================================================
! SAO LUU VA KHOI PHUC CAU HINH QUA TFTP (y 35)
! May chu: Server-Backup 192.168.0.11
! Chuan bi: Server-Backup > Services > TFTP > On
! Ten file: <hostname>-confg-<yyyymmdd>
! Thuc hien sau MOI lan thay doi cau hinh, tren ca 8 thiet bi.
! =====================================================================
!
! ---------- Sao luu (che do enable) ----------
copy running-config tftp:
! Address or name of remote host []? 192.168.0.11
! Destination filename [R-CH1-confg]? R-CH1-confg-20261020
! -> ket qua mong doi: !! va [OK - ... bytes]
!
! Kiem tra: Server-Backup > Services > TFTP -> co du 8 file
!
! ---------- Khoi phuc ----------
copy tftp: running-config
! Address or name of remote host []? 192.168.0.11
! Source filename []? R-CH1-confg-20261020
! Destination filename [running-config]? <Enter>
!
! ---------- Luu y voi switch cua hang ----------
! Switch gui TFTP tu IP 192.168.x0.2, di qua G0/0 chieu IN cua router cua hang.
! ACL cua TV2 can co dong sau, dat TRUOC dong chan POS -> Server-Backup:
!   permit udp host 192.168.10.2 host 192.168.0.11 eq 69
!   (tuong tu cho 192.168.20.2 va 192.168.30.2)
